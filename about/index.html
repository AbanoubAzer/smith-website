About SMITH Co. LTD

Your Partner in Architectural Products and Global Trade

Who We Are

A Global Trading Partner You Can Rely On

We are a general trading business based in China, serving customers and businesses seeking architectural products, building materials, and reliable sourcing solutions for international markets.

Our experience includes sourcing doors, aluminum windows, wrought ironwork, pergolas, kitchens, quartz, marble, and other products for residential and commercial projects. Beyond our product range, we support clients through supplier coordination, order follow-up, inspection arrangements, export documentation assistance, and international shipping coordination.

With our China-based operations, regional representatives, and U.S. installation partners, we aim to provide a more connected and convenient experience for customers across different markets.

Our Mission

To simplify international sourcing and trade by connecting customers with suitable products, suppliers, and logistics solutions.
