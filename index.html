FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Sourcing With EITCO

Transparent answers regarding factory auditing, order customization, inspections, shipping routes to the USA and Gulf, and payment coordination.

Our China office team in Hangzhou conducts direct on-site factory audits before any agreement. During and after manufacturing, we carry out comprehensive pre-shipment inspections covering dimensions, structural tolerances, finish consistency, hardware operation, packaging, and crate reinforcement.

Yes. Our local partner office, Modish Kitchen Reno in New Jersey, provides a U.S. point of contact for eligible product orders, project requirement reviews, agreed local collection, and payment coordination. Contact us to verify eligibility for your specific project.

Standard ocean freight transit times are approximately 25 to 35 days to U.S. ports (East or West Coast) and 18 to 25 days to Arabian Gulf ports (Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, UAE). Air freight is coordinated for samples or urgent orders within 5 to 8 business days.

Yes, customized dimensions, finishes, glass specifications, and hardware are our core specialty. We coordinate shop drawings and material samples directly with verified manufacturers to ensure complete compliance with local building codes.

We provide commercial invoices, detailed packing lists, ocean bills of lading (B/L), certificates of origin (CO), and test certificates as required. We assist your designated customs broker in the USA or destination country for smooth clearance.

You can submit your specifications directly through our Request a Quote form, send technical drawings to eliagadallah@eit-co.com, or message our international trade team immediately via WhatsApp at +86 186 1623 2842.
