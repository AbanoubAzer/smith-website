What We Do

End-to-End Sourcing Support

We source products according to your specifications, coordinate with trusted suppliers, and handle inspections, documentation, and shipping — so you can focus on your business.

Product Sourcing

We help you find the right products matching your specifications, design references, and budget from our supplier network.

Supplier Coordination

We handle communication with suppliers on your behalf — from initial inquiry to order confirmation and production follow-up.

Order Inspection

Pre-shipment inspection arrangements to verify product quality, specifications, and quantity before your order leaves the factory.
