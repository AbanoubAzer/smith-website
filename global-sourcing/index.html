Global Sourcing & Trade Services

Your Product. Our Network. From supplier selection to final delivery.

What We Do

End-to-End Sourcing Support

We source products according to your exact specifications, coordinate with trusted suppliers, and handle inspections, documentation, and international shipping — so you can focus on growing your business.

🔍

Global Product Sourcing

We help you find the right manufacturers and products matching your specifications, technical drawings, and target budget from China.

🏭

Supplier Audits & Coordination

We manage direct communication with verified factories — from initial technical inquiries to sample approval, price negotiation, and production tracking.

🔬

Order Inspection & Quality Control

Rigorous pre-shipment inspections covering dimensions, materials, finish tolerances, packaging, and labeling before container loading.

📋

Export Documentation & Customs Support

Assistance with commercial invoices, packing lists, bill of lading, certificates of origin, and compliance paperwork required for customs clearance.

🚢

Sea & Air Freight Coordination

End-to-end international freight coordination (FCL & LCL container shipping, air cargo) with dependable carrier options and competitive rates.

🔧

Local Handover & Partner Support

Through our U.S. partner network in New Jersey, New York, and Tennessee, we coordinate local handover and installation support for eligible orders.

Our 5-Step Sourcing Process

01

Share Your Requirements

Tell us what you need — product type, dimensions, drawings, finish, quantity, target budget, and destination.

02

Supplier Research & Quotation

We evaluate suitable manufacturers from our verified network and coordinate detailed quotations and material samples.

03

Order Follow-Up & Coordination

We monitor factory production timelines, milestone schedules, and technical compliance.

04

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Quality control inspectors verify dimensions, finish, quantity, packaging, and crate labeling before release.

05

Export, Shipping & Handover

We assist with customs paperwork, coordinate ocean/air freight, and coordinate with our U.S. partner network for eligible orders.
